# Student Guide — LCC E-Portal

## Logging in

You'll log in with your **Student ID**, not an email address — the system never asks for one, and if
anything ever does, it isn't really this system. Your Admin office gives you a temporary password in person
or on paper the first time. The first thing you'll be asked to do is set a real password; you can't skip
this or reach anything else first.

## Your home page

After logging in you'll see your name, Student ID, department, enrolment year, and the current semester and
its state. If your course plan needs something from you — you haven't started it yet, it was sent back for
revision, or it's waiting on Admin approval — you'll see a single line telling you so.

## Course planning

From your home page, go to **Course planning** to build your plan for the upcoming semester: pick your
courses, submit for approval. If a prerequisite can't be confirmed yet (common early on, while the Admin
office is still entering historical records), an Admin can override it for you — ask your Admin office if
you're stuck at this step.

## Your academic record

Your home page shows, per semester: courses taken, credit hours, grade, and your semester GPA, plus your
cumulative CGPA, academic standing, and credits earned toward the 132 needed to graduate. If your historical
record is still being entered by the Admin office, you'll see a **Provisional** notice — your figures may
still change until that's marked complete. You can print your semester record from the page; doing so is
logged.

A grade only ever appears here once it's been fully approved and published — you will never see a grade the
moment an instructor enters it, only once the review step is done.

## Grading policy

**Grading policy** (linked from your home page) shows the current grade scale — what each letter grade is
worth in grade points, and which grades count toward your GPA.

## If something looks wrong

Talk to your Admin office. Every correction to a locked grade requires a second person's decision and a
recorded reason — even the Admin office can't fix it unilaterally on the spot, so a real correction may take
a day or two to go through properly.

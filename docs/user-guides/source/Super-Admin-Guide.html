Good to know

  • You are the second pair of eyes. Admins enter grades; nothing reaches students until you approve it.
  • You can look at students, plans and offerings, but the day-to-day editing belongs to the Admins.
  • At least one active Super Admin must always exist. The system refuses to disable the last one.
  • Everything you decide is recorded with your name in the audit log.

1

Approve a class’s grades

Check what an Admin submitted and publish it to the students.

WhereGradesGrade submission reviewWhenever the dashboard shows submissions waiting
1

Open the review queue

Each line shows a class and how many grades are undecided.

Click Review on the class you want.

2

Read the table

Columns: Student, Grade, Status. Look for anything that seems wrong.

3

Choose who to decide

Tick rows to decide only those. Tick none to decide the whole class.

4

Approve

Click Approve checked (or all, if none checked).

The grades are published and GPAs update.

5

Or reject

Type the Reason for rejection (required), then click Reject checked (or all, if none checked).

The grades return to the Admin as drafts.

6

Done

The class leaves the queue when nothing is left undecided. Others show “(partially decided)”.

Where your decision sends a grade
SubmittedBy the Admin
ApprovePublished, students see it
RejectBack to Draft with your reason
A submission is decided only once. After approval, a grade changes only through a correction request (next task).
2

Decide a grade correction

Approve or refuse a change to a grade that is already published.

WhereGradesGrade correctionsWhen a correction is pending
1

Open Grade corrections

Scroll to Pending correction requests. An empty list says “No corrections awaiting a decision”.

2

Compare

Each request shows the old grade, the new grade and the Admin’s reason.

3

Approve

Click Approve. The grade, the student’s GPA and the transcript update at once.

4

Or reject

Type a Note and click Reject. Nothing changes for the student.

Admins cannot approve their own requests; the decision is yours alone.
A request cannot be decided twice: the page answers “This request is already APPROVED”.
3

Create, disable or enable an Admin account

Give a new colleague access, or remove it the moment someone leaves.

WhereAccountsAdmin accountsWhen staff join or leave
1

Open the form

On Admin accounts find Create Admin / Super Admin account.

2

Fill it in

Username, Display name and Role (Admin or Super Admin).

3

Create

Click Create account. A green box shows the temporary password once.

4

Hand it over

Give the password to the person directly. At first sign-in they must choose their own (at least 10 characters for staff).

5

Disable when needed

In Existing accounts, click Disable in the Action column. They are locked out immediately, even if signed in.

6

Enable again

Click Enable on a disabled account to give access back.

The temporary password is shown once. If it is lost, create a new account or ask IT to reset it.
You cannot disable the last active Super Admin. Create another one first.
4

Move a semester forward, or reopen a closed one

Start or end a term, and undo a Close when something was missed.

WhereAcademicAcademic calendarSemesters tableTerm start, term end, or a rare correction
1

Find the semester

In the Semesters table look at the Change state column.

2

Choose the next state

In Move to pick the next state. For a Closed semester the only choice is In Progress (reopen).

3

Reason

Normal moves: a Reason is optional for you.

Reopening always needs a reason, and it is saved in the audit log first.

4

Confirm

Click Change state, or Reopen semester for a reopen.

Who may do what
Draft → OpenAdmin or Super Admin
Open → In ProgressAdmin or Super Admin
In Progress → ClosedAdmin or Super Admin
Closed → In ProgressSuper Admin only, with a reason
Only one semester can be In Progress. Close the other one before reopening an old semester.
Creating and deleting semesters stays with the Admins.
5

Read the audit log

See who did what and when, and what changed.

WhereAccountsAudit logTo investigate or to review a period
1

Open Audit log

Newest activity is first. Opening the page is itself recorded.

2

Narrow it down

Use Action, Entity type, Student ID (internal), From and To, then click Apply.

3

Open an entry

Click View change to see the value before and after.

4

Clear the filters

Click Clear filters beside Apply to start again.

Logins, passwords and temporary passwords are never stored in the log. Prints and exports are.
6

Download a whole semester

Get final grades and results out of the system, as a file or a print.

WhereGradesSemester exportEnd of term reporting
1

Open Semester export

The table lists every semester, its students and grades recorded, and Not yet published counts.

2

Pick the file

Course grades: one row per student per course (grade, grade point, credit hours). Semester results: one row per student (credits, semester GPA, CGPA, standing).

3

Or print

Print / PDF gives a printable version.

A copy of the College’s academic data leaves the system with every file. Each download and print is recorded in the audit log.
Locked out of everything? Another active Super Admin can create or enable your account. Keep at least two Super Admins so this never blocks the College.

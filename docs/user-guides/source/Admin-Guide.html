Good to know

  • Two people, two jobs. You enter grades and request corrections. A Super Admin approves them before students can see anything.
  • Every action is written to the audit log, including prints and exports.
  • You are signed out after 1 hour without activity.
  • A temporary password is shown once. Hand it over straight away.

1

Enrol a new student

Create the student’s record and account in one form.

WhereStudentsStudent ListingFor each new admission
1

Open Student Listing

Click Add Student at the top right. The Enrol a student form opens.

2

Fill the required boxes

Student ID (digits, starting with the admission year, e.g. 202634), First name, Last name, Gender, Enrolment year.

The year must match the Student ID.

3

Choose College, then Department

Pick College first. The Department list unlocks and shows only that college’s departments.

4

Optional details, then enrol

Middle name, Minor and Phone can stay empty.

Click Enrol student.

5

Copy the login details

A green box shows the Student ID and the Temporary password.

Give both to the student, or print a login slip (next task).

The temporary password is shown once and is not stored in readable form. If it is lost, use Reset password on the student’s profile.
Past grades, photo and transcript details (date of birth, county, parent and so on) are added later from the student’s profile.
2

Give students their login details

Print slips with Student ID and a new temporary password, or reset one password.

WhereStudentsLogin slipsAt registration, or when a student is locked out
1

Tick the students

Open Login slips, filter by department if you like, and tick the students.

At most 60 per print.

2

Start the print

Click Print slips for N students. A box asks you to confirm.

3

Confirm

Click Yes, issue all N. Each student gets a new temporary password, so any older slip stops working.

4

Print and hand out

Click Print slips, cut the slips and give each to its student.

Leave the page and the passwords cannot be shown again. A lost slip needs a new one.
Just one student? Open Student Listing, open the student, and click Reset password. The temporary password appears on the screen, once.
3

Set up a new academic year and semester

Create the calendar entries before the term starts.

WhereAcademicAcademic calendarBefore each year and each semester
1

Add the academic year

Under Academic years type Label like 2026/2027, then Start date and End date.

Click Add academic year.

2

Add a semester

Under Semesters choose the Academic year and the Name (Semester I or II).

Enter Start date and End date, inside the year.

3

Create it

Click Add semester. It appears in the table as Draft: setup only, invisible to students.

A Draft semester is the only one you can delete (red Delete this semester button). Once it is Open it can only move forward.
4

Change a semester’s state

Move the semester through its life: Draft, Open, In Progress, Closed.

WhereAcademicAcademic calendarSemesters tableAt the start of planning, the start of the term and the end of the term
1

Find the semester

Open Academic calendar and look in the Semesters table. The Change state column shows what is allowed next.

2

Choose the next state

In Move to pick the next state. There is only ever one choice, because moves go forward only.

3

Give a reason

As an Admin, Reason (required) must be filled in, for example “Registration opens Monday”.

4

Confirm

Click Change state. The table shows the new state.

The change is written to the audit log.

Semester life and what it allows
DraftSetup only. Students see nothing. Can be deleted.
OpenStudents plan courses. You review plans.
In ProgressTerm running. Late adds and drops. Grades are entered.
ClosedSealed and read-only.
Only one semester can be In Progress at a time. Close the old one first.
A Closed semester cannot be reopened by an Admin. Ask a Super Admin, who must give a reason.
5

Review and approve course plans

Decide the plans students have submitted; approving creates their registrations.

WherePlanningCourse plan reviewWhile the semester is Open
1

Pick the semester

Open Course plan review, choose the semester and click Select.

Search by Student ID or name if you need one plan.

2

Open a plan

The list shows Student ID, Name, Submitted and who entered it. Click Review plan to see every course.

3

Read the warnings

Red or amber boxes warn about seats, prerequisites and timetable clashes.

An override needs a written reason, which is kept on the record.

4

Approve

Click Approve beside a course, or Approve all.

5

Or reject with a reason

Click Reject, type the reason and confirm. Reject all turns down the whole plan and also needs a reason. The student sees it and can resubmit.

6

Registrations created

Approved courses become registrations. They show on Registrations and in the class lists.

A student cannot plan? Use Course plan entry to build and submit the plan for them. Late add or drop? Use Registrations: Register or Drop always asks for a reason.
6

Print a student’s official transcript

Produce the College transcript for a student.

WhereStudentsTranscriptsOn request
1

Find the student

Open Transcripts. Type a name or ID in Student ID or name and click Search.

2

Open the transcript

Click Transcript beside the student. The full transcript shows, every semester in order, with totals.

3

Print or save

Click Print or save as PDF, then choose a printer or Save as PDF.

The same page shows a yellow warning if the student’s past records are still being entered. Prints are recorded in the audit log. Need a whole semester? Use Semester export.
7

Enter class grades and send them for approval

Type each student’s score, save a draft, then submit the class.

WhereGradesClass grade entryOnly while the semester is In Progress
1

Choose the semester

Open Class grade entry, pick the Semester and click Select.

2

Choose the class

Pick the Class and click Select. The class list appears.

3

Type the scores

Fill Score for each student: 0 to 100, digits only, one decimal at most (74.5). The letter shows beside it.

Tick Incomplete instead of a score if needed.

4

Save the draft

Click Save draft. Save as often as you like; nothing is final yet.

5

Submit the class

In Submit for approval click Submit.

Missing grades? Tick Confirm submitting with missing grades and add the Note.

6

Wait for the Super Admin

The grades are locked for you. Once approved, students can see them.

Life of a grade
DraftYou can change it
SubmittedWaiting for the Super Admin
PublishedStudents see it
RejectedBack to Draft, with the reason
A typo like 101, abc or 59,5 stops the save with an error. Fix it and save again. A mistake found after publishing needs a correction (next task).
8

Correct a published grade

Ask for a change to a grade students can already see.

WhereGradesGrade correctionsAfter a grade is published
1

Find the class

Open Grade corrections, choose Semester and Class. Published grades are listed.

2

Open the request

Click Request a correction beside the student.

3

Say what and why

Type the New score (or tick Incomplete) and a clear Reason. The reason is required.

4

Send it

Click Request. It joins Pending correction requests for the Super Admin.

Once approved, the grade, GPA and transcript update by themselves. Only one request per grade can be pending.
9

Import past grades

Bring in old semesters from the paper grade sheets, by file or by hand.

WhereStudentsImport past gradesWhile loading the old records
1

Choose how

Two tabs: Upload CSV for many sheets, Enter by hand for one student’s semester.

2

Upload: check first

Choose the grade-sheet file and click Check the file. Nothing is saved yet.

Each sheet is checked against students, courses, calendar and its printed GPA.

3

Read the result

Ready to import sheets passed every check. Held back sheets are not imported. Download the problem list, fix the file, check again.

4

By hand: pick the student

Search the student, choose the Academic year and Semester (it must be over), then enter each course, credit hours and letter from the paper sheet (use Add row for more courses).

5

Check against the paper

Click Check before saving. The GPA is worked out and anything that disagrees with the paper is shown.

6

Import or save

Tick the confirmation and click Import N sheets, or by hand Save N grades.

Only sheets that pass every check are imported. A held-back sheet imports nothing, so one bad row never half-saves a student.
Something does not work? Note the screen, the student’s ID and what you clicked, then tell the Super Admin. Never share a password by message.
